Why your photos lose GPS

      

        Android 13+ strips location data from any photo your apps pick from the gallery — including web apps like this. The photo still has GPS in Google Photos, but the version handed to Fauna doesn't. There's no fix from inside the app: the bytes simply arrive without coordinates.
      

      
The fix that always works

      
Share the photo to Fauna instead of picking it from inside Fauna. Share intents send the original file with GPS intact.

      
One-time setup

      
    
        
  1. In Chrome on this device, tap the menu (⋮) → Install app (or "Add to Home Screen"). Open Fauna once from the home-screen icon to register it as a share target.
    2. 
      

      
Adding a sighting after that

      
    
        
  1. Open Google Photos (or any gallery app) and tap the photo you want to log.
    2. 
        
  2. Tap Share → pick Fauna from the share sheet.
    3. 
        
  3. Fauna opens with the photo loaded, GPS auto-filled, and the map pinned to where you took it.
    4. 
      

      
For new sightings in the field

      
Tap the Camera button instead — Fauna uses your device's current location, which is just as accurate as photo GPS.
